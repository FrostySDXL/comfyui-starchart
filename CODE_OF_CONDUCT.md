# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, constructive place for contributors,
reviewers, and users.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that help build a positive environment include:

- showing respect for different experience levels and viewpoints
- giving actionable, technical feedback without personal attacks
- assuming good intent while still being clear about problems
- focusing discussion on the work, the evidence, and the proposed change

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- doxxing or attempts to expose private information
- bad-faith disruption, intimidation, or repeated hostility
- publishing security-sensitive details in public when private reporting is more appropriate

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull
requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns related to repository participation, open a private
maintainer contact path where available. For security-sensitive reports, follow
the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
